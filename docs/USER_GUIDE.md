# AgroConnect User Guide

AgroConnect is an inclusive, accessible marketplace platform designed for Kenya's agricultural ecosystem. Visitors can explore produce, inputs, and services immediately without creating an account. An account is only required when posting listings or placing orders.

---

## 1. User Roles Overview

| Role | Target Audience | Primary Capabilities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Guest / Visitor** | Public visitors | Browse produce, browse inputs & services, filter by category and county, add items to cart |
| **Farmer** | Smallholder & commercial farmers | Post produce harvests, manage inventory/pricing, view buyer orders, fulfill orders, receive inquiries |
| **Supplier** | Agro-dealers, vets, agronomists | Post farm inputs (seeds, fertilizer, kits) and services (vet visits, soil testing), manage stock/slots, fulfill orders |
| **Buyer** | Consumers, bulk buyers, restaurants | Browse all catalogs, place orders, cancel pending orders, contact sellers with custom inquiries |

---

## 2. Farmer User Journey

### Step 1: Create a Farmer Account
1. Open AgroConnect and click **Sign in** in the top navigation header.
2. Select **Create an account**.
3. Enter your **Full name**, **Phone number**, **County**, and choose **Farmer** as your account type.
4. Optionally specify your farming focus (e.g. *Horticulture and dairy*).
5. Enter your email and a secure password (minimum 8 characters), then click **Create account**.

### Step 2: Post Produce for Sale
1. Navigate to the **Produce market** tab.
2. Click **+ Post produce** in the top right.
3. Fill in listing details:
   - **Listing name** (e.g., *Grade 1 Field Tomatoes*)
   - **Category** (e.g., *Vegetables*)
   - **Price in KSh** & **Per unit** (e.g., *85* per *kg*)
   - **Quantity available** (e.g., *320*)
   - **County** (e.g., *Nakuru*)
   - **Availability** (e.g., *Harvesting this Friday*)
   - **Description** of quality, grading, and pickup/delivery options.
4. Click **Publish listing**. Your produce is immediately live in the public market.

### Step 3: Manage Listings & Orders
1. Open the **My activity** tab.
2. In the **Listings** tab, click **Edit** or **Delete** on your existing harvest listings.
3. In the **Orders** tab, review incoming purchase requests from buyers.
4. Click **Confirm** when preparing the order, then **Mark fulfilled** once collected or dispatched.
5. In the **Messages** tab, view direct buyer inquiries and questions.

## 3. Supplier User Journey

### Step 1: Register as a Supplier
1. Click **Sign in** &rarr; **Create an account**.
2. Select **Input or service supplier** as the account type.
3. Enter company/practitioner details and location.

### Step 2: Post Farm Inputs or Practical Services
1. Navigate to **Inputs & services** and click **+ Post offering**.
2. Select the listing type:
   - **Farm input**: For physical supplies such as seeds, fertilizers, feeds, or irrigation equipment.
   - **Service**: For consultations, veterinary farm visits, soil tests, or tractor hiring.
3. Provide unit pricing (e.g., *KSh 4,500 / set* or *KSh 1,800 / sample*) and current stock or booking slots.
4. Click **Publish listing**.

---

## 4. Buyer User Journey

### Step 1: Explore and Filter
1. Switch between the **Produce market** and **Inputs & services** tabs.
2. Use the **Search bar** to find specific crops, inputs, or brand names.
3. Use the **Category** dropdown to narrow by agricultural category (e.g., *Cereals*, *Fertiliser*, *Veterinary*).
4. Use the **County** dropdown to find local produce near your location.

### Step 2: Inquire with Sellers
1. Click the **Contact** button on any listing card.
2. Write a message subject and body (e.g., asking for bulk discounts or special delivery arrangements).
3. Click **Send message**. The seller will see your message in their activity center.

### Step 3: Shopping Cart & Atomic Checkout
1. Click **+ Add to order** on any available produce, input, or service.
2. Open your order drawer by clicking the **Order (Basket)** icon in the header.
3. Adjust quantities using the `+` and `−` controls.
4. Review the total price in Kenyan Shillings (KES).
5. Click **Place order**.
6. Track order progress under **My activity** &rarr; **Orders**. If needed, you can cancel an order while it is in `pending` status.
